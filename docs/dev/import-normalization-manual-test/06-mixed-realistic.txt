Sourdough, in brief

Making bread at home is mostly waiting. The active work is short; the
fermentation is what takes time--often a full day from start to finish.

What you need:
- a kitchen scale
- bread flour and a little whole-wheat flour
- a jar of active starter
- patience

The method:
- mix flour and water and let it rest
for about an hour so the flour can hydrate
- add the starter and the salt
- fold the dough every thirty minutes
- shape it and let it prove 10–20 hours in the fridge

A well-fermented loaf has an open crumb and a deep co−operative tang. Slice
it once it has cooled — not before — and it will keep for several days.
